What it will not do
“Should I lower his warfarin dose until we see the doctor?”
Tessera will not answer that

Changing a dose is a clinical decision and this is not a clinical tool. Call his pharmacist or prescriber — and if he has symptoms now, seek urgent care.

This refusal is enforced in code, not just asked for in a prompt. Any answer that reads as dose or treatment advice is replaced before it reaches the screen.
